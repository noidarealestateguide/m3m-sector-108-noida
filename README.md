# M3M Sector 108 Noida

**M3M Sector 108 Noida** is an upcoming mixed-use development planned across a prime **12.5-acre land parcel in Sector 108, Noida**. The development is planned to include residential and commercial components, making it an upcoming destination for both living and business opportunities.

## M3M Sector 108 Noida Highlights

* Prime location in Sector 108, Noida
* Approx. 12.5-acre land parcel
* Upcoming mixed-use development
* Residential and commercial components
* Located along the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway corridor
* Connectivity towards Delhi and Greater Noida
* Upcoming M3M development

## Location & Connectivity

M3M Sector 108 is positioned within the established Noida Expressway corridor. The location provides road connectivity towards Delhi, Greater Noida and other important areas of the NCR. The wider area also has access to the Aqua Line metro network and surrounding residential, healthcare, education and commercial infrastructure.

## Residential & Commercial Development

The planned development is positioned as a mixed-use destination combining **residential and commercial opportunities**. The residential component is expected to offer a premium urban living environment, while the commercial component may provide opportunities for retail, business and office spaces.

## Price & Availability

The official selling price, residential configurations, commercial inventory, floor plans and detailed master plan are currently subject to official release. The current project information lists the price as **On Request**.

The reported **₹1,839 crore** figure refers to M3M's winning bid for the 12.5-acre land parcel and should not be considered the selling price of the project.
